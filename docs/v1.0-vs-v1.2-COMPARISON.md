## FAQ

### General

**Q: Which should I choose?**  
A: If timeline allows, **choose v1.2**. The 6 features have clear business value (reviews = +conversions, EMI = +order value, reminders = +retention). +1 week is standard for quality e-commerce. v1.0 is only if you have a hard 3.5-week deadline.

**Q: What's the actual ROI of v1.2 features?**  
A: Rough estimates (conservative):
- **Reviews:** +20% conversion = +₹100–200k/month revenue
- **EMI:** +10% order value (on 30% of orders) = +₹20k/month
- **Reminders:** +3–5% recovery = +₹10–20k/month
- **reCAPTCHA:** Avoids spam/fraud cleanup costs (~5% of support time)
- **Total:** ~₹150–250k/month revenue upside for +₹8k investment = 18–31x ROI over 12 months

### Timeline

**Q: Why does v1.2 take 4.5–5 weeks instead of exactly 4 weeks?**  
A: Week 4 is lighter (polish, not heavy CRUD), but we include 3–5 day final QA buffer. Realistic delivery is 4.5–5 weeks. If you need exactly 4 weeks, we can defer translator + GMB (both cosmetic) and hit Week 4.

**Q: Can we compress v1.2 into 3.5 weeks?**  
A: Not recommended. That's 51 dev-days in 18 calendar days (6-day weeks). Quality + testing suffers. Contingency buffer vanishes. If critical, defer 3 cosmetic features (translator, GMB, EMI UI polish) and ship v1.0 + reviews + reCAPTCHA.

### Pricing

**Q: Why is the offer price not discounted more for v1.2?**  
A: Offer discount is 27.27% (v1.2) vs 28.89% (v1.0) — nearly identical. The ₹8k premium is fair for 10 additional dev-days (₹800/day competitive market rate). Estimation (list price) went from ₹45k → ₹55k because effort actually increased.

**Q: What if I only want 2–3 of the 6 features?**  
A: We can create a custom v1.1 scope. Example: reviews + reCAPTCHA + translator (10 days) = ₹35–37k offer, 4-week timeline. Contact vendor to discuss.

### Features

**Q: Will reviews hurt our reputation if customers leave bad reviews?**  
A: Reviews are moderated (admin approval). Negative reviews on legitimate issues are OK (builds authenticity + trust). Spam/fake reviews are rejected. Net effect: mostly good reviews, some honest criticism, zero fake content.

**Q: Is reCAPTCHA annoying for users?**  
A: v3 is **invisible**. Real users don't see it or notice it. Only obvious bots get blocked silently. No friction for legitimate customers.

**Q: Can customers get EMI on any order amount?**  
A: No. Razorpay + partner banks set minimums (~₹3,000–₹5,000) and check eligibility (customer credit score). EMI option shows "Apply for EMI" at checkout; ineligible customers just pay in full. Automatic + no manual work.

**Q: Does translator break mobile design?**  
A: No. Translator widget is responsive (fits top-right on mobile). Tested on all screen sizes. May be hidden under hamburger menu on very small phones.
